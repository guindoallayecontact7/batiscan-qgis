about=BatiScan turns building points collected in the field into building footprints using Segment Anything (MobileSAM / SAM ONNX, no PyTorch needed). It also offers a click-to-map tool, automatic detection with any Deepness-compatible ONNX segmentation model, a points-vs-footprints quality report, and export of image/mask tiles to train a model for your own area. Works with GeoTIFF orthophotos and XYZ/WMS basemaps. Requires the Python packages onnxruntime and opencv (installed by the plugin on first use). Designed for rural and peri-urban Sahel settlements (round huts and rectangular roofs).
